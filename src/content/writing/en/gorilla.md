I went to view two places in a certain prefecture.

Both had private rooms with a shared kitchen. Very self-improvement-minded places, where you keep your privacy and still get to enjoy mingling with the other residents. I met the agent at the station, and we checked the entrance, the private room, the shared shower and then the shared kitchen. So I wouldn't miss anything, I fired the questions I'd prepared in Google Calendar at the agent. How soon can I move in, can I take out the trash every day, until when does the campaign apply, are utilities included in the rent: I listed everything that might trip me up after moving in and asked. I asked about everything that bothered me. Then the agent asked whether I wanted to put a provisional hold on a place before signing. I said let me think about it.

By normal standards both looked like great places. The second one especially was amazing. A brand-new building, built in 2020. You can buy food and drinks at the supermarket on the ground floor and cook in your room. You can drink on the rooftop terrace with a Ferris wheel in the distance as your backdrop. In the shared space, which looks just like a Recruit office, residents can enjoy pool and games together. You can cook in the kitchen built into your own room, and work at your desk looking out over the city at night. An appartement that achieves both privacy and the encouragement of communication at a very high level.

But a discomfort that stayed with me stopped me from signing. Residents who looked moderately good at their jobs, with rude attitudes. A woman's voice, cheerful but menacing, like a mountain gorilla. A man's swaggering Kansai dialect, squeezed out so as not to be outdone. This cacophony leaked out of the living room next door. I thought I'd put a lid on what stinks, and here I was about to jump right into it. Let's imagine. Once they knew my name and I became one of the crowd, I'd be shouting from the pit of my stomach so it reached every corner of the room, just to show I exist. To show I'm one of them, I'd react to topics I don't care about with a stupidly loud laugh. I'd notice someone in the group had a crush on someone and be careful not to get in the way. And so I'd become a gorilla shrieking inside the in-group too, and then waste away alone in my room.
