The infrastructure is amazing. But the longer I stay, the more I start to feel like I'm useless to society, like garbage. Why is that? Maybe it would be different if I joined some mutual-aid group outside of work. But this is probably structural. Selective ignoring. The more I soak in Japan, the more my place disappears. If someone had reached out to me, I wouldn't have left the country. Moving abroad wasn't something I grabbed for myself; it was something I had to do. Natural selection. I'm on the side that doesn't get selected.
